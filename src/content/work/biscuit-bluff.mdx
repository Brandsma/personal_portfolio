Initially inspired by Balatro, and adding some casino-themed risk mechanics creates a compelling formula for deckbuilding games. **Biscuit Bluff** takes this foundation and adds persistent progression through a shop system where players use earned "biscuits" to buy new card packs between runs, creating a fun gameloop.

For Brackeys Game Jam 2025's "Risk it for the Biscuit" theme, we had a full week but could only dedicate evening hours around work schedules. This constraint led us to focus on two core elements: a solid simple gameplay loop inspired by successful deckbuilders and polished game feel that makes every interaction satisfying.
