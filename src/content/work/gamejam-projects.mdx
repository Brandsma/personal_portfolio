## Shadow Rush

_Ludum Dare 50 · Theme: "Delay the Inevitable" · 72 hours_

Our first jam. Shadow Rush is a top-down racer where your previous laps come back as ghost cars chasing you around the track. The better you get, the more ghosts pile up, until your past self inevitably catches you. We also somehow shipped a working community level editor within those same 72 hours, which I'm still proud of. It placed **67th in Fun** out of thousands of entries.

![Action shot of the car sliding around a corner with ghost cars visible in the background](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMTQ3MjkyMC84NTg5NjI5LnBuZw==/original/UBaEMX.png)

_[Play Shadow Rush on itch.io](https://the-human-person.itch.io/shadow-rush) · [Level editor](https://shadow.jossafossa.nl/)_

## Peter OS

_Ludum Dare 51 · Theme: "Every 10 Seconds" · 72 hours_

A smartphone simulator where a notification hits Peter's ancient phone every ten seconds. Players juggle messages, crashing apps and RAM warnings across a set of lovingly parodied apps: SnapCat, SuddenKilo, Harmony. Writing little text conversations between characters was genuinely fun, and the pixel UI came out great. It placed **74th in Humour** and **75th in Innovation**.

![Peter's home screen showing colourful custom app icons and the RAM monitor widget](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMTczMjAzNC8xMDE5NjIzNS5wbmc=/original/qxZYz2.png)

_[Download Peter OS on itch.io](https://the-human-person.itch.io/peter-os-ludum-dare-51) (Windows, Mac, Linux)_

## Don't Let Them Bug You

_Ludum Dare 52 · Theme: "Harvest" · 72 hours_

A tower defense game where you can harvest your own plant defenders for seeds to grow new ones. Sacrifice a level 3 watermelon now, or let it keep shooting? The tension between immediate firepower and future growth made for a surprisingly interesting strategy loop. It was also our first time using Blender and 3D in general, which shows, but people still had fun with it. **177th in Theme**.

![The farm layout with low-poly plants in their plots and bugs approaching along the dirt path](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMTg3MDkxOC8xMDk5MDM0MC5wbmc=/original/Kq4ZF7.png)

_[Play Don't Let Them Bug You in browser](https://mantsje.itch.io/dont-let-them-bug-you)_

## Morgue Mayhem

_Ludum Dare 54 · Theme: "Limited Space" · 72 hours · ⚠️ morbid humour_

Overcooked but you're morticians. Up to four players assemble corpses from mismatched body parts to match what grieving families asked for, then cram them into undersized coffins. Our first time using Godot, and our first time with a six-person team. Both went fine. **187th in Graphics**.

![Graveyard overview showing players navigating between graves, storage areas and the incinerator](https://img.itch.zone/aW1nLzEzNjA1NjIxLnBuZw==/original/T956MG.png)

_[Play Morgue Mayhem in browser](https://sjmdev.itch.io/morguemayhem) - up to 4 players locally, controllers recommended_

## Vocatio

_Ludum Dare 55 · Theme: "Summoning" · 72 hours_

A bullet-hell wizard game where casting spells requires drawing gestures with your mouse: circles for fire, pentagrams for healing, lightning bolts for targeted damage. Getting the gesture recognition working accurately under pressure was the fun part. Getting players to learn all the patterns in 10 seconds was the mistake. **133rd in Theme**.

_[Download Vocatio on itch.io](https://mantsje.itch.io/vocatio) (Windows, Mac, Linux - no web build due to threading constraints)_

## Kinder Guardin'

_Ludum Dare 56 · Theme: "Tiny Creatures" · 72 hours · ⚠️ morbid humour_

You're a teacher. The children are in an unsafe environment. Armed with candy, chalk, homework and emergency puppy deployment, you try to keep as many alive as possible through a school day. Do well, and next level your class gets bigger. This one landed **26th in Humour**, our best placement in that category.

![Rainbow victory screen celebrating a day with only one child lost](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzAyNDk5Ny8xODA5MzQ1OS5wbmc=/original/K%2FlpBl.png)

_[Play Kinder Guardin' in browser](https://mantsje.itch.io/kinder-guardin)_

## Biscuit Bluff

_Brackeys Game Jam 2025 · Theme: "Risk it for the Biscuit" · one week (evenings only)_

A Balatro-inspired deckbuilder where you push your luck through score targets to earn biscuits. You can cash out and take them to the shop for new cards, or keep going for more. Bust a round and you lose everything you've accumulated. People kept playing it way longer than expected, which is the highest compliment a jam game can get.

![The main gameplay interface showing cards, score tracking, and the "Maybe next time" card ready to play](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzg0OTk2MC8yMjk2MTQxOC5wbmc=/original/oIMp0L.png)

_[Play Biscuit Bluff in browser](https://the-human-person.itch.io/biscuit-bluff) - also on Windows, Mac, and Linux (better audio on desktop)_

## Looble

_GMTK Game Jam 2025 · Theme: "Loop" · 5 days (2–3 days effective)_

A puzzle platformer where a tiny soap drop bounces through bathroom bubbles. Some teleport you to the opposite side of the bubble, others attach to you and spin you around until they pop. We built the levels in LDtk and were happy with how the puzzles came out. Then we submitted with a game-breaking bug 30 seconds before the deadline. The fixed version is below.

![Gameplay showing the soap drop character near bubbles and platforms in a bathroom setting](https://i.postimg.cc/7hXJHMN3/image.png)
