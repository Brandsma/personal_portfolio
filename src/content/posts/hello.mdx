I've started and abandoned this blog four times. Each time the failure mode was the same: I tried to make every post a Statement, hated the result, and stopped writing.

This is attempt five. The only rule is *published > polished*. If a post would be useful at 70% and unbearable at 100%, ship it at 70.

I expect to write about: research I'm working on, things I'm learning in production ML, thoughts on the field, occasional non-AI things. Mostly I expect the audience to be future me. If anyone else finds it useful, that's a bonus.

See you on the other side of attempt five.
